Research

My research centers on learning-based robotic manipulation, world models, autonomous driving, and the security of cyber-physical systems. I build methods that guide a robot's actions at test time and improve the safety of autonomous vehicles, and I develop dynamic-watermarking techniques that secure networked control systems against attacks.

ReGuide: From Test-Time Guidance to Self-Improving Diffusion Policies
Tzu-Hsiang Lin, Srinivas Shakkottai, Dileep Kalathil, and P. R. Kumar
arXiv, 2026

Phase-conditioned test-time guidance for diffusion policies, paired with a self-improvement loop that collects guided rollouts, filters successes, and fine-tunes the base policy—improving success by 1.2–7.2× on Robomimic Can, Square, Transport, and Tool Hang.
